# Job Tracker: brief

## Who uses it

An person with a degree who is eager to land a well paid job

## Core Jobs

Track: keep track of all job posts/applications
Move: User can has many posts status, like interested, wish list, applying, ...etc
Search: user can search the job market to find a suitable job
Notes: user can attach notes in his applications, like resources and cvs
Next actions: set a follow-up with a due date and see what is due today.

## Non-goals

- No real email: the app never sends or reads email.
- No multi-user sharing: one user, no accounts, no collaboration.
